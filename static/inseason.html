Only show
How to read this

Winner = which team gains more over the window, and by how much. Me / Them = change in each lineup's total over the window. Per-game = blended value you gain or give up long term; a window gain with a negative per-game is a schedule play that costs you later.

Shop a player

How to read this

Every 1-for-1 for that player, ordered by how the deal looks to a typical manager (% rostered across Yahoo, this season's Fan Pts and pre-season rank; weights in config/inseason.yaml): top = looks like you win (harder sell), bottom = looks like they win.

Winner = who really wins and by how many points: your lineup simulated night by night through the end of your fantasy playoffs (playoff games count extra, playoff_game_weight). A player who'd sit on your bench adds nothing. They'll see = who a typical manager would think wins. ★ = you gain, but to them it looks even or in their favour: the ones worth proposing. Fit = change in each lineup over the window (positional need).

Trade with a team

How to read this

Every 1-1, 2-1, 1-2 and 2-2 package with one team. Winner = which team really wins and by how many points, simulated through the end of your fantasy playoffs. Taking back more players than you send means cutting the ones who add least (shown under the deal); a deal that opens a roster spot fills it with the best free agent for your lineup.

They'll see = who a typical manager would think wins. ★ = you win and they should see it as even or a win for them. Next N days = whose lineup gains more over the window. Too close = margin under 10% of the value changing hands. Under each name: Yahoo injury tag, plays = share of games played/started if under 85%, PO = games in your fantasy playoffs.

Tick players (up to 2 a side) to see only deals that include them, best for you first (untick "only ones they'd likely take" to see the losing ones too). Tick both sides to also score that exact offer.
